    <p>I’m Chengyan Tsai, a gameplay and tools programmer and technical artist focused on Unreal Engine, real-time graphics, and production workflows. I like building systems that feel responsive in play and make complex work easier for designers and artists.</p>

    <p>I recently completed a Master of Computer Science at Texas A&amp;M University, where I worked with the Soft Interaction Lab on CineDesk, a virtual-production interface accepted to the SIGGRAPH 2026 Posters program, and an AI-driven forensic nursing training simulation.</p>

    <p>Previously, I worked at Winking Studios on Project Kyzen and an unannounced AAA action RPG, developing gameplay systems, dialogue tools, and reusable interaction frameworks. I’m now looking for opportunities in gameplay programming, tools development, and technical art.</p>
